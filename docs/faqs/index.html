General

What is FlashBit Financing Corp.?

FlashBit Financing Corp. is a Philippine financing company operating under the names of Livaya Financing, Alago Financing and Sulivo Financing.

Is FlashBit registered with the SEC?

Yes. FlashBit Financing Corp. is registered with the SEC (Registration No. 202609066778-02) and holds Certificate of Authority No. F-26-0144-55. We are also registered with the Anti-Money Laundering Council (Registration No. 20260924204248-7).

When will loan products be available?

The planned launch date is December 1, 2026, subject to confirmation.

Application

Who can apply for a loan?

The proposed general requirements include Filipino citizenship, residence in the Philippines, age 21 or older, a valid government-issued ID and a regular source of income. Requirements may vary by product.

What documents should I prepare?

You may need a government-issued ID, proof of address and proof of income, such as payslips, a certificate of employment or business documents. Additional documents may be requested depending on the product. See What you’ll need.

How long does the review take?

Review timing depends on verification and the information needed for your application. If approved, you will receive an offer with the loan amount, costs and repayment schedule.

Payments

How will I receive the loan proceeds?

After you accept a loan offer, proceeds can be sent to a bank account or supported e-wallet in your name. Check your offer for the available disbursement method.

Where can I see the total cost?

Before accepting, review the interest, fees, charges and total amount to repay in your offer and Disclosure Statement. See Interest, Fees and Charges in the Terms and Conditions.

Data Privacy

How is my personal information protected?

FlashBit limits the personal data it collects to what is needed for the stated purposes and uses safeguards to protect it. Read the Privacy Notice or contact the Data Protection Officer at dpoflashbit@gmail.com.
