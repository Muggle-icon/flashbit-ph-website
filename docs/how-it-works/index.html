How It Works

See each step from application to the release of funds. Applications are planned through the financing apps.

  1. 1

    Apply Online

    Choose a financing product and complete the application when it becomes available.

  2. 2

    Review and Verification

    The company reviews your application and verifies the information you provide.

  3. 3

    Get a Loan Offer

    If approved, you receive an offer with the amount, costs and repayment schedule.

  4. 4

    Review and Accept

    Review the Disclosure Statement and Terms and Conditions before accepting.

  5. 5

    Receive Funds

    After acceptance, funds are sent through the disbursement method shown in the offer.

Always read the Terms and Conditions before proceeding in the loan transaction.

Review the disclosures, costs and repayment responsibilities before accepting a loan.

What you'll need

Prepare these before you start. Additional documents may be requested depending on the product.

  • Valid government ID

    Any one (1) valid government-issued ID.

  • Proof of income

    Payslips, certificate of employment, or business documents.

  • Bank or e-wallet account

    A supported account in your name to receive loan proceeds.

  • Eligibility

    Filipino citizen living in the Philippines, aged 21 or older.
