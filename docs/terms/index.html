These Terms and Conditions govern your use of the website and services of FlashBit Financing Corp., operating under the names Livaya Financing, Alago Financing, Sulivo Financing.

1. Eligibility

To be eligible to apply for a loan or financing product from FlashBit Financing Corp., an applicant must meet the following requirements:

  • At least 21 years old at the time of application
  • Filipino citizen and currently residing in the Philippines
  • Has at least one (1) valid government-issued ID
  • Has a regular and verifiable source of income
  • Has an active Philippine mobile number and valid email address
  • Has an active bank account or other supported disbursement account under the applicant's name
  • Must provide complete, accurate, and truthful information and supporting documents required for identity verification, credit assessment, and regulatory compliance

Important Reminder

Meeting the minimum eligibility requirements does not guarantee loan approval. All applications are subject to identity verification, credit assessment, applicable lending criteria, and the internal policies of FlashBit Financing Corp.

2. Loan Application and Approval

Applying for a loan is simple, secure, and convenient.

How to Apply

  1. Complete Your Application

    Fill out the online loan application form and provide complete and accurate personal, employment, income, and other required information.

  2. Submit the Required Documents

    Provide a valid government-issued ID and any other documents that may be required for identity verification and credit assessment.

  3. Verification and Credit Assessment

    Your application will undergo identity verification, eligibility checking, and credit assessment based on the lending criteria and policies of FlashBit Financing Corp.

  4. Review Your Loan Offer

    If approved, you will receive the applicable loan offer showing the approved amount, interest rate, fees and charges, repayment schedule, and other important terms and conditions.

  5. Accept the Loan Terms

    Carefully review the Disclosure Statement, Terms and Conditions, interest, fees, charges, and repayment obligations before accepting the loan.

3. Interest, Fees and Charges

The applicable interest rate, effective interest rate, fees, charges, penalties, and total amount payable will be clearly disclosed to the borrower before the completion of the loan transaction.

Interest rates, fees, and other charges shall be imposed in accordance with applicable laws, rules, and regulations of the Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) and other relevant regulatory authorities.

4. Repayment and Late Payment

The Borrower shall repay the loan, including applicable interest, fees, and charges, on or before the agreed due date. In case of late payment or non-payment, a penalty not exceeding 5% per month of the outstanding scheduled amount due may be imposed, subject to applicable SEC rules. The total interest, fees, charges, and penalties shall at all times remain within the applicable regulatory ceilings.

5. Fair Debt Collection Policy

FlashBit Financing Corp. is committed to conducting all collection activities in a fair, respectful, transparent, and lawful manner.

In the event of late payment or non-payment, the Company may contact the Borrower through authorized communication channels for purposes of providing payment reminders, discussing the outstanding obligation, and facilitating appropriate payment arrangements.

The Company and its authorized collection personnel or third-party service providers shall not use threats, intimidation, harassment, abusive or insulting language, false or misleading representations, or other unfair debt collection practices.

Collection activities shall respect the Borrower's privacy and confidentiality. Personal information shall not be unnecessarily disclosed to persons who are not authorized or legally entitled to receive such information.

Where applicable, penalties for late payment or non-payment shall not exceed the ceilings prescribed by the Securities and Exchange Commission and other applicable laws and regulations.

Borrowers experiencing difficulty in making payment are encouraged to contact FlashBit Financing Corp. through its official customer service channels to discuss their account and available payment arrangements.

All collection activities shall be undertaken in accordance with applicable SEC regulations, consumer protection requirements, data privacy laws, and other relevant Philippine laws and regulations.

6. Data Privacy

Personal Data We Collect

FlashBit Financing Corp. ("FlashBit," "we," "us," or "our") may collect and process personal data that is necessary for evaluating loan applications, verifying identity, providing financing services, servicing and collecting loans, preventing fraud, complying with legal and regulatory requirements, and providing customer support.

Depending on the services used, we may collect the following categories of personal data:

1. Personal and Identification Information

  • Full name
  • Date of birth and age
  • Sex
  • Nationality
  • Civil status
  • Residential and/or mailing address
  • Mobile number and email address
  • Valid government-issued identification and identification number
  • Photograph or selfie submitted for identity verification

2. Employment and Financial Information

  • Employment or occupation
  • Employer or business information
  • Source of income
  • Monthly or estimated income
  • Bank account or other authorized disbursement and repayment account information
  • Information relating to existing financial obligations, where necessary and lawfully obtained

3. Loan and Transaction Information

  • Loan application details
  • Requested and approved loan amount
  • Loan tenor
  • Interest, fees, and charges
  • Disbursement information
  • Repayment history
  • Outstanding balances
  • Late or missed payments
  • Collection and payment-arrangement records

4. Credit and Verification Information

Subject to applicable law and appropriate authorization, we may obtain information necessary to assess identity, eligibility, creditworthiness, fraud risk, and the accuracy of information provided in a loan application.

5. Device and Technical Information

When you access our website or Online Lending Platform, we may collect technical information reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention, authentication, and operation of the service, such as:

  • IP address
  • Device type and operating system
  • Browser or application information
  • Device and session identifiers
  • Login and transaction records
  • Date and time of access
  • Security and fraud-prevention information

6. Communications and Customer Service Information

We may maintain records of communications with you, including inquiries, complaints, requests, payment reminders, collection communications, and other correspondence made through our authorized customer service and communication channels.

Data Minimization

FlashBit shall collect only personal data that is adequate, relevant, suitable, necessary, and not excessive in relation to the declared and specified purposes for which the information is processed.

We shall not require access to personal information or device permissions that are unnecessary for the provision of our financing services. Personal data shall be processed in accordance with applicable Philippine data privacy, consumer protection, financing, lending, and other regulatory requirements.

Purpose of Processing Personal Data

FlashBit Financing Corp. ("FlashBit," "we," "us," or "our") collects and processes personal data only for legitimate, specific, and declared purposes related to the provision of our financing services.

  1. Loan Application and Eligibility Assessment

    To receive, process, evaluate, and determine your eligibility for a loan or other financing product.

  2. Identity and Information Verification

    To verify your identity, contact information, employment, income, and other information submitted in connection with your application.

  3. Credit Assessment and Risk Management

    To assess creditworthiness, repayment capacity, fraud risk, and other risks relevant to the evaluation and management of your loan.

  4. Loan Approval and Disbursement

    To process approved loans, execute loan-related transactions, and facilitate the disbursement of loan proceeds through authorized channels.

  5. Loan Account Administration and Repayment

    To maintain and administer your loan account, process repayments, monitor outstanding balances, provide account information, and maintain transaction records.

  6. Payment Reminders and Debt Collection

    To send payment reminders, communicate regarding overdue obligations, facilitate payment arrangements, and undertake lawful and fair debt collection activities.

  7. Fraud Prevention and Security

    To detect, investigate, prevent, and manage suspected fraud, identity theft, unauthorized transactions, security incidents, and other unlawful activities.

  8. Customer Service and Complaint Handling

    To respond to inquiries, requests, concerns, complaints, disputes, and other customer service matters.

  9. Legal and Regulatory Compliance

    To comply with applicable laws, regulations, lawful orders, and regulatory requirements, including requirements of competent government and regulatory authorities.

  10. Recordkeeping, Audit, and Internal Control

    To maintain appropriate business and transaction records and support compliance monitoring, risk management, internal controls, and authorized audits.

  11. Protection and Enforcement of Legal Rights

    To establish, exercise, protect, or defend the lawful rights and interests of FlashBit, its customers, and other authorized parties.

Personal data shall not be processed in a manner incompatible with the declared, specified, and legitimate purposes for which it was collected. FlashBit shall process only personal data that is necessary and proportionate to the applicable purpose and shall implement appropriate safeguards in accordance with applicable data privacy laws and regulations.

Sharing and Disclosure of Personal Data

FlashBit Financing Corp. ("FlashBit," "we," "us," or "our") respects the confidentiality of your personal data. We do not disclose or share personal data except when necessary for legitimate business purposes, the performance of our contractual obligations, compliance with legal or regulatory requirements, or as otherwise permitted by applicable law.

Subject to appropriate safeguards and applicable data privacy requirements, we may share or disclose personal data to the following:

  1. Service Providers and Third-Party Service Providers (TPSPs)

    Authorized service providers that support our operations, including information technology and cloud service providers, identity and fraud verification providers, payment and disbursement service providers, customer service providers, and other contracted service providers.

  2. Banks and Payment Service Providers

    Banks, electronic payment providers, payment gateways, and other authorized financial service providers as necessary to facilitate loan disbursements, repayments, refunds, and reconciliation.

  3. Credit Information and Credit-Related Entities

    Credit bureaus, credit information entities, and other authorized institutions, where permitted or required by law, for purposes such as credit assessment, verification, credit reporting, and risk management.

  4. Authorized Collection Service Providers

    Where necessary, personal data may be disclosed to duly authorized collection agencies or service providers for lawful collection of outstanding obligations. Such disclosure shall be limited to information reasonably necessary for the collection activity and shall remain subject to applicable data privacy and fair debt collection requirements.

  5. Government and Regulatory Authorities

    Personal data may be disclosed to the Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC), Anti-Money Laundering Council (AMLC), Credit Information Corporation (CIC), National Privacy Commission (NPC), courts, law-enforcement agencies, and other competent government authorities when required or authorized by applicable law, regulation, lawful order, or regulatory requirement.

  6. Professional Advisers and Authorized Representatives

    We may disclose information to our authorized legal counsel, auditors, accountants, consultants, and other professional advisers when reasonably necessary for legitimate business, compliance, audit, or legal purposes.

  7. Corporate Transactions

    Subject to applicable legal requirements and appropriate safeguards, information may be disclosed when reasonably necessary in connection with a merger, acquisition, restructuring, financing, investment, transfer of assets, or similar legitimate corporate transaction.

Protection of Shared Personal Data

FlashBit shall disclose only personal data that is necessary and proportionate to the purpose of the disclosure. Where required, recipients and service providers shall be subject to appropriate contractual, organizational, technical, and security safeguards designed to protect the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of personal data.

FlashBit shall not sell, rent, or disclose personal data to third parties for unauthorized purposes.

Personal data shall not be disclosed publicly or to unauthorized persons for purposes of embarrassing, harassing, pressuring, or publicly shaming a borrower in connection with the collection of a debt.

Where personal data is transferred or processed outside the Philippines, FlashBit shall implement appropriate safeguards and comply with applicable Philippine data privacy requirements.

Retention of Personal Data

FlashBit Financing Corp. ("FlashBit," "we," "us," or "our") shall retain personal data and relevant records for a period of five (5) years, or for such longer period as may be required or permitted by applicable laws, regulations, regulatory authorities, or legitimate legal purposes.

The retention period shall be applied in accordance with FlashBit's Records Retention Policy and applicable regulatory requirements.

Personal data may be retained beyond the five (5)-year period when necessary for an ongoing investigation, complaint, dispute, litigation, regulatory examination, legal hold, or the establishment, exercise, or defense of legal claims.

Upon expiration of the applicable retention period and when there is no longer a lawful or legitimate purpose for continued retention, personal data shall be securely deleted, destroyed, anonymized, or otherwise disposed of using appropriate physical, organizational, and technical safeguards.
