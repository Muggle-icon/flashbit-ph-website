# Layout and grouping

These rules record the 2026-09-25 review of Flashbit and its three product pages.

- A section is a semantic grouping, not an instruction to draw a panel. Do not place every section inside the same white rounded box on a gray canvas.
- Company page: open two-column introduction, a broader community photograph, then three distinct brand destinations. Only those destinations use cards; each whole card is one link. End with a dark utility footer.
- Product pages: full-width brand-colored everyday-purpose introduction; quieter white borrowing section; soft brand-colored app guide; white utility footer. Preserve this reading order at every width.
- Borrowing information uses an open heading and responsibility note beside one compact checklist. Fine horizontal dividers separate its three related facts. Do not frame the complete section or create one card per fact.
- The app guide has open text/selectors beside one contained screenshot preview. Start both columns at the top, use natural step heights, and keep the original screenshots synchronized with the only selector set. On mobile, the active description belongs below the screenshot and controls.
- Rhythm comes from changes in role, width, background and density. The normal content maximum is 1120px; the company photograph can reach 1280px. Major desktop sections use 64px spacing, with smaller gaps for headings and related content. These values are project decisions, not universal rules.
- Borders separate related items and footer utilities. Do not restore a decorative stripe below the header, underline selected controls, or add nested cards without a distinct purpose.
- Preserve brand assets, colors, natural local imagery, financial information boundaries, visible keyboard focus and reduced-motion support.

Sources: [IBM Carbon spacing](https://carbondesignsystem.com/elements/spacing/overview/), [Microsoft Fluent card](https://fluent2.microsoft.design/components/web/react/core/card/usage/), [Microsoft Fluent divider](https://fluent2.microsoft.design/components/web/react/core/divider/usage).

These pages combine open content, broad media or color fields, and contained interactive content. They inform composition, not a universal spacing formula, a claim of local user validation, or permission to copy product promises.

Before release, review all four pages at desktop and mobile sizes, including section transitions and real carousel states. Check the deployed GitHub Pages version. Passing dimensions and contrast checks alone does not establish visual quality or local user validation.
